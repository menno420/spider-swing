Privacy Policy for Slingy Spider

Last updated: 23 August 2026

The short version

Slingy Spider does not collect your data. It does not send anything about you, your device, or how you play to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics, or third-party trackers.

What the game stores

The game saves progress on your device, including settings, best distances, upgrades, cosmetics, in-game currency, reached regions, and recent run information. This stays in Android's private storage for the game. We cannot see it and it is not uploaded.

Uninstalling the game removes this local save. If you choose Copy JSON in Run History, the visible run record is put on your device clipboard so you can paste it yourself. The game does not send it anywhere.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not show ads or use an advertising identifier.
  • It does not contain analytics, crash reporting, or tracking.
  • It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or files.
  • It does not sell or share information about you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a later version adds an online feature such as leaderboards, this policy and the Google Play Data safety information will be updated before that version is released.
