# Zones 3–8 finished-art source record

> **Status:** `production-source-record`

Twenty original assets were generated by OpenAI image generation on 2026-07-31
and 2026-08-01. The only visual context consulted was Spider Swing's own
committed runtime art: Ancient Forest for the original finish direction, then
the approved Silk Hollow and Ruined Arboretum backdrop/obstacle assets for the
seven foreground corrections. No external image, museum object, paper figure,
stock asset, Wikimedia file, or other third-party reference was consulted or
copied; therefore there is no reference URL, creator attribution, or external
licence to record.

Every source was generated on a flat chroma field, keyed to alpha at its full
generation resolution with the image-generation skill's
`remove_chroma_key.py`, then reduced with a Lanczos filter. The runtime matte
was despilled again after resize. `tools/zone_art_audit.py` inspects partial
alpha at keyed-source, runtime, and 25% gameplay scale. The committed audit
reports zero visible magenta or green key fringe in all three states. Full
source canvases are represented by SHA-256 rather than committed, matching the
existing forest art policy; Godot imports only inspected runtime files.

## Shared generation direction

- painterly naturalistic fantasy finish compatible with the existing game;
- large structural silhouettes readable at 760 px lead and at 25% scale;
- subdued background detail; valid anchors, lethal rims, and flies remain the
  highest-contrast elements;
- no text, UI, player, fly, watermark, frame, or raster-defined collision;
- flat `#ff00ff`-family chroma field, or green for the three new warm Silk
  objects where magenta would collide with their pink fibres, later keyed to
  alpha;
- seam-aware compositions mirrored on alternating tiles by `SwingLabView`.

## Generation specifications and hashes

The original generation specifications were: vaulted cocoon-and-lattice hollow;
isolated cocoon/spindle cluster; ruined greenhouse gables and mechanisms;
articulated hanging glass pane; open slate ridge with uniformly bent trees;
isolated marked rock spire; cathedral silk bridges; long-legged warning-colour
resident; fractured burnt hollow; perforated rotten branch; solid sound char
branch; near-empty mist landmarks; and one cyan-banded mist beam.

The 2026-08-01 correction used image-reference generation (not third-party
reference material), pairing each request with its approved in-repository zone
backdrop and prior obstacle finish. Prompts requested isolated, orthographic
game objects on flat chroma: one woven cocoon, one long silk spindle, one
horizontal lattice strut, one broken greenhouse beam, one symmetric two-ended
rotor arm, one circular corroded hub, and one solid filled collapsed frame. The
collapsed-frame result was edited once to replace a false open arch with opaque
glass, mesh, and debris matching its solid collision polygon. Every prompt
excluded text, UI, player characters, flies, scenery, cast shadows, borders,
and watermarks.
