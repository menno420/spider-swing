## Recurring problems + fixes

- **Contract counts come from the executable runner, never PR prose.** PR #89's
  closeout said 176 after its first replay batch, while its final merged
  `tests/test_runner.gd` expected and executed 181. Run the exact gate and copy
  its group totals into living docs.

## Past mistakes to avoid

- **Do not turn several owner recordings into one environment rewrite.** The
  PR #90 task grew from wall art into four zones, shared renderer paths,
  parallax, support geometry, a 33-asset audit, Android export, and publication.
  It merged green, but owner feedback arrived too late and the session stayed
  visibly active after completion. For visual work: diagnosis-only first, prove
  shared changes in one zone, one zone per PR/APK, wait for owner-device
  acceptance, then continue. Close the chat handoff immediately after merge.
