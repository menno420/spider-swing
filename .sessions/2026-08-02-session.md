**Findings that were new to the repository, all verified this session:**

1. **Ancient Forest is the one region excluded from the recovery cadence.**
   `course_pattern_catalog.gd:288-299` gives every other region an
   `open_recovery` chunk every fifth local chunk — Bramble gets one every
   *other* chunk — and gives Ancient Forest none, inserting a difficulty-4
   `tight_rail` after 2 km instead. Its own region metadata
   (`course_region_catalog.gd:38`) declares the quirk "Wide recovery rhythm".
   Corroborated independently: `open_recovery` never appears in the 0 m band of
   the difficulty-curve measurement's death table, because there is none to die
   on. This is the strongest available structural explanation for the 2–5 km
   wall.
2. **The early-course thresholds are pixels, so they are ten times earlier than
   previously reported.** Control pool 1 km, mastery pool and tight corridors
   2 km, deep pool 3.5 km, obstacle growth fully saturated at 3.5 km. A prior
   session read these as 10/20/35 km; the correction is recorded in § 2.2 and
   § 4.2 of the new document.
3. **Warning time shrinks as skill rises, from two independent systems** —
   fixed 700–820 px hazard cues (`simulation_world.gd:507`) and a fixed 853 px
   camera preview with no zoom (`swing_lab.gd:243-247`).
4. **Zero drive also disabled the motor's overspeed correction**
   (`spider_motor.gd:22-27`) — both branches scale by
   `horizontal_drive_acceleration`, so air drag is now the sole decay above the
   reference band.
5. **Compact Stance's floor first binds at L36**, making levels 37–40 paid
   no-effect purchases.
6. **Reliable Launch and Anchor Drive compound against fine control**:
   `requested_travel = max(anchor_distance × fraction, minimum)`, so the range
   in which the floor overrides a proportional pull grows from 200 px to 437 px
   between L0 and L40, at twice the radial speed.

## 💡 Session idea

**The bridge between headless measurement and device-only skill already exists
in source, unfinished.** `SwingLabSession.toggle_recording` already records a
human's input commands and `export_diagnostic()` already writes them out with
the seed and resolved config; `tools/simulate.gd --replay` and
`load_input_trace` already replay the `spider-swing-input-trace@4` format
deterministically, within a contract-enforced one metre. The two payloads differ
in format only. Closing that gap would let the owner's *own* runs be replayed
headlessly under the full lab instrumentation — per-chunk survival, preview time
at death, escape-held-at-death, release-quality histogram, bird-gap history —
and would retire the project's largest measurement blind spot without fitting a
bot at all. Every document in `docs/measurements/` currently works around the
bot's inability to pump; this makes that irrelevant for anything the owner has
personally played.

## ⟲ Previous-session review

The previous session card recorded "HEAD unchanged, nothing committed yet" and
was correct to: that session was a read-only analysis whose only artifact lived
outside the repository. The workflow cost is that its findings — including the
10×-off reading of the early-course thresholds, corrected here — were never
exposed to the doc-hygiene gate or to a reviewer, so an error survived a whole
session inside a document nobody could check.

**Workflow improvement:** when a session's deliverable is an analysis rather than
code, write it into `docs/` and let the normal gate see it. A read-only session
is a reason to change no *source*, not a reason to leave the conclusion
unreviewable. Two of the six findings above are corrections to earlier analysis
that only surfaced because an external review read the same code independently —
which is an argument for making analysis reviewable by default.
